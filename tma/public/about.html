❓ How to use

Forward a suspicious image or link to @NahThatsFakeBot, or start a check with the /check command. You get AI-assisted image/deepfake analysis and multi-provider URL safety checks.

Free plan includes daily credits. Useful bot commands: /credits (balance), /refer (earn bonus credits), /history (recent checks), /premium (plan information).
